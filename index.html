Research

Research paper

Multisensor AI for Identifying Contaminated Recyclable Materials

Trash Vision AI: a proposed intelligent approach for smarter waste sorting.

Sense→Fuse→Analyze→Decide→Sort
Read the idea

A camera alone can’t see what a bottle is made of or what is left inside it, and even a little contamination can get a whole batch of recyclables rejected.

The paper proposes combining four sensors (an RGB camera, a NIR/hyperspectral sensor, a moisture/thermal sensor and a metal sensor). Their readings are fused to estimate the material, check contamination and give a confidence score, then the item is accepted, cleaned, inspected or re-sorted.

It is a concept, not a working machine yet: real data, a prototype and proper testing would come first.
